# Project OysterPod

## Overview

**Founder**
May 2023 – May 2024

**Stack:** Linux, Bash

## What I Did

I founded and led Project OysterPod, an initiative to provide offline-first access to educational software in areas without reliable internet connectivity. This effectively became a distributed fleet of edge nodes operating with zero reliance on a central network.

I configured Linux environments running the Kolibri learning management system to operate fully offline at the edge, packaging curated educational content directly onto each device so students could learn without any dependency on an internet connection. I deployed and managed this fleet across more than 40 laptops spanning Metro Atlanta and rural India, coordinating logistics across two very different deployment environments.

To make that scale feasible, I wrote automated OS-flashing and package-provisioning scripts that standardized fleet-wide provisioning, cutting per-device configuration time by 60% and eliminating the manual, error-prone process of configuring each edge node individually. The project combined distributed-systems thinking, systems administration, scripting, and logistics into an end-to-end effort, from provisioning software to getting hardware into students' hands.
