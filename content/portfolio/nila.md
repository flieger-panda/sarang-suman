# IoT Facility Management Platform

**Startup Project**
Oct. 2025 – Dec. 2025

**Stack:** React, Firebase, MQTT

I engineered an MVP for an IoT-enabled facility management platform, featuring a real-time MQTT pub/sub architecture for device-state syncing and remote monitoring. I also designed scalable Firestore data models and built an AI-powered floorplan-scanning pipeline to automate customer onboarding.
